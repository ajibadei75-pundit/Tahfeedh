NOOR HIFZ — deploy
==================
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/projects/noor-hifz/deploys
2. Drag noor-hifz-site.zip onto the deploy area.
3. Open https://noor-hifz.netlify.app and hard-refresh once (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+R).

Existing accounts are asked to choose their gender once on next sign-in
(students are matched only with scholars/teachers of the same gender).
